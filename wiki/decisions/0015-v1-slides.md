# Decision

1. **Intro:** total matches and hours in the Cursed City.
2. **Your main:** most-played hero, its win rate, and a global percentile.
3. **Roster:** number of heroes played, with a one-trick or flex label.
4. **Scavenger card:** three stats on one card. **Crate Goblin** (breakables) top-left, **Gambler** (Sinner's Sacrifice) middle-right, **Urn Mule** (idols returned) bottom-left.
5. **How you kill:** gun, ability, melee, and headshot kills, plus close-up versus long-distance kills.
6. **Precision:** accuracy against heroes, headshot rate, and parry king or clown. (This slide may merge into 5; see [slide details](0021-kill-slides-and-mvp.md).)
7. **Mid Boss:** Rejuvenators claimed.
8. **Roast:** death heatmap ("your favorite place to die") plus hoarded unspent souls.
9. **Rank journey:** badge over time.
10. **MVP / signature stat:** the single most interesting or unique thing about the player, inspired by Overwatch's MVP screen. The selection logic is in [slide details](0021-kill-slides-and-mvp.md).
11. **Summary card:** exportable.

v2 candidates: most-bought item, fastest core item.
