# Summary

The City Never Sleeps update (live 2026-09-29) rebuilt the map.[^cns] The M3 style preview copies it in low poly. Images are hot-linked in the preview's mood board and never committed (ADR 0068).

# Layout

| Lane | Colour | Stops (from the Hidden King's side) |
|---|---|---|
| York (left) | Yellow | Stock Exchange, Seaport Dock, Old Fairfax Factories |
| Broadway (middle) | Blue | Uptown, Times Square, Downtown |
| Greenwich (right) | Green | Umbria University, Central Park, The Brownstones |

- **Districts** between the lanes: Theater, Chinatown, Haunted Lot, Plaza.[^cns]
- **Bases:** one at each end, and the lanes converge in front of them.
  - The Hidden King's base is a run-down factory (amber).
  - The Archmother's base is a neoclassical museum (sapphire).[^wiki-map]
- **Zip lines:** each lane's zip line runs base to base overhead, in the lane's colour.[^wiki-map]
- **Defences per lane, per team:** a Guardian, a Walker, then two Base Guardians.[^wiki-map]
  - Guardians are squat pale robots standing on green ritual circles (Uptown screenshot).

# Landmarks

- **Bridges:** each lane has one.
  - Broadway: a stone overpass over Times Square.
  - Greenwich: an arched stone footbridge in Central Park.
  - York: a steel rail trestle at the Seaport.
- **Mid boss:** a hunched, sack-bodied ghoul holding up a glowing teal eye.
  - It lives in a sewer pit under Times Square, reached through entrances marked with the Rejuvenator sign on Broadway.[^wiki-midboss]
  - The update did not move it.
- **Look:** dusk light (a blue-grey sky with a warm horizon).
  - Broadway: neon signs and billboards.
  - Greenwich: overgrown and lit with green lamps.
  - York: industrial, with sandstone and rust colours.[^cns]
